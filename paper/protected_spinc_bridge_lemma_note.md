# Protected Spin^c Bridge Lemma Note

## Scope

This note is not part of the submission baseline and does not modify
`paper/main.tex`, the global scan, or any baseline figure.  It isolates the
optional post-submission question:

> Can the protected \(Spin^c\) index sector
> \(H^0(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O(2))\) be bridged to the existing PSLT
> \(N=1,2,3\) spectral-layer projectors without retuning the scan?

The answer from the current artifacts is deliberately conservative:

1. the index side gives an exact three-dimensional protected flag,
2. the existing G1 artifact gives an audited finite-volume spectral flag of
   ranks \(1,2,3\),
3. therefore a dimension/flag bridge exists under the stated hypotheses,
4. a canonical operator-level intertwiner is still open because no
   anchor-overlap artifact is currently present.

## Index-Side Data

Assume the protected internal curve and generation line bundle are

\[
C\simeq\mathbb{CP}^1,\qquad
R=p_+ + p_-,\qquad
L_R=\mathcal O(R)\simeq\mathcal O(2).
\]

The positive \(Spin^c\) Dolbeault-Dirac kernel is

\[
\ker D_R^+
\simeq H^0(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O(2)).
\]

Riemann-Roch on \(\mathbb{CP}^1\) gives

\[
h^0(\mathcal O(2))-h^1(\mathcal O(2))
=\deg \mathcal O(2)+1=3.
\]

Serre duality gives

\[
h^1(\mathcal O(2))
=h^0(K_{\mathbb{CP}^1}\otimes\mathcal O(-2))
=h^0(\mathcal O(-4))=0.
\]

Thus

\[
h^0(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O(2))=3,\qquad
h^1(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O(2))=0.
\]

In affine coordinate \(\zeta\),

\[
H^0(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O(2))
=\mathrm{span}\{1,\zeta,\zeta^2\}.
\]

This carries the natural degree flag

\[
F_1=\mathrm{span}\{1\}
\subset
F_2=\mathrm{span}\{1,\zeta\}
\subset
F_3=\mathrm{span}\{1,\zeta,\zeta^2\},
\]

with

\[
\dim F_N=N,\qquad N=1,2,3.
\]

## Spectral-Side Data

The current G1 certificate constructs, for each audited separation \(D\), a
finite-volume self-adjoint generalized eigenproblem

\[
K_D u=\lambda M_Du,\qquad M_D>0,
\]

and its ordinary self-adjoint representative

\[
H_D=M_D^{-1/2}K_DM_D^{-1/2}.
\]

When the first three eigenvalues are strictly ordered,

\[
\lambda_1(D)<\lambda_2(D)<\lambda_3(D),
\]

the cumulative projectors

\[
E_N(D)=\mathrm{Ran}\,{\bf 1}_{(-\infty,\lambda_N(D)]}(H_D)
\]

form a spectral flag

\[
E_1(D)\subset E_2(D)\subset E_3(D),
\qquad
\dim E_N(D)=N.
\]

The existing artifact
`output/gn_fp_2d/gn_lowN_microstate_count_summary.csv` reports, on the fine
audited grid,

\[
\min_D(\lambda_2-\lambda_1)=4.164426355\times10^{-2},
\]

\[
\min_D(\lambda_3-\lambda_2)=4.968688976\times10^{-2},
\]

with zero rank error and exact cumulative ranks \(1,2,3\).

## Bridge Lemma

Let

\[
V_{\rm ind}=H^0(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O(2))
\]

with the degree flag \(F_1\subset F_2\subset F_3\).  Let

\[
V_{\rm sp}(D)=E_3(D)
\]

with the spectral flag \(E_1(D)\subset E_2(D)\subset E_3(D)\).  On any audited
\(D\)-interval where the gaps

\[
\lambda_2(D)-\lambda_1(D)>0,\qquad
\lambda_3(D)-\lambda_2(D)>0
\]

remain open, there exists a smooth family of flag-preserving unitary maps

\[
U_D:V_{\rm ind}\to V_{\rm sp}(D)
\]

such that

\[
U_D F_N=E_N(D),\qquad N=1,2,3.
\]

Equivalently, the protected index flag can be paired with the current
finite-volume PSLT low-mode flag.

### Proof

The index side has the orthogonal one-dimensional quotient lines

\[
G_N=F_N\cap F_{N-1}^{\perp},\qquad F_0=0,
\]

generated by \(1,\zeta,\zeta^2\) after choosing the standard Fubini-Study
Hermitian structure.

The spectral side has the quotient lines

\[
L_N(D)=E_N(D)\cap E_{N-1}(D)^{\perp},\qquad E_0(D)=0.
\]

Because \(H_D\) is self-adjoint and the first three eigenvalues are simple,
Kato perturbation theory gives smooth spectral projectors on any
gap-preserving \(D\)-interval.  The audited \(D\)-domain is an interval and
therefore contractible, so the three eigenline bundles \(L_N(D)\) are
topologically trivial.  Choose smooth normalized sections \(u_N(D)\in L_N(D)\).
Define

\[
U_D(\zeta^{N-1})=u_N(D),\qquad N=1,2,3,
\]

and extend linearly and unitarily.  Then \(U_DF_N=E_N(D)\) for every
\(N=1,2,3\).  This proves the flag bridge.

The construction is unique only after fixing the phase of each \(u_N(D)\), or
more generally after choosing an anchor convention.  Without such an anchor,
the bridge is canonical as a flag identification but not as a unique
operator-level intertwiner.

## Promotion Condition Not Yet Met

A full parent-side promotion would require an explicit embedding or overlap
operator

\[
T_D:H^0(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O(2))\to\mathcal H_{\rm FV}(D)
\]

and a certified \(3\times3\) overlap matrix

\[
A_{nk}(D)=\langle u_n(D),T_D e_k\rangle,
\qquad
e_k\in\{1,\zeta,\zeta^2\},
\]

such that the protected index sector lands in the same three spectral layers.
A sufficient certificate would be

\[
\sigma_{\min}(A(D))>0
\]

together with a tail bound

\[
\|P_{>3}(D)T_D\|<\epsilon
\]

and a fixed phase/anchor convention across the audited \(D\)-window.

No such anchor-overlap artifact is currently present.  Therefore the result is

\[
\boxed{\text{dimension/flag bridge: yes; canonical promotion: not yet}.}
\]
