## Parent-Side Unit Flux Input

The PSLT two-center conformal source uses two identical normalized center
profiles.  For the Plummer regulator

\[
\rho_\epsilon(r)=
\frac{3\epsilon^2}{4\pi(r^2+\epsilon^2)^{5/2}},
\]

one has

\[
\int_{\mathbb R^3}\rho_\epsilon\,d^3x
=
\int_0^\infty
\frac{3\epsilon^2 r^2}{(r^2+\epsilon^2)^{5/2}}\,dr.
\]

With \(r=\epsilon t\),

\[
\int_0^\infty
\frac{3t^2}{(1+t^2)^{5/2}}\,dt
=
\left[
\frac{t^3}{(1+t^2)^{3/2}}
\right]_{0}^{\infty}
=1.
\]

Hence each center carries one normalized source unit.  The V4 lock interprets
this unit source as one projective ramification/flux unit on the internal
curve.  This is the precise conditional input:

\[
q_+=q_-=1.
\]

## Divisor Lock

On the V3 curve \(C\simeq\mathbb{CP}^1\), let \(p_+\) and \(p_-\) be the two
faithful marked centers.  A general effective divisor supported on these two
centers is

\[
R_{m_+,m_-}=m_+p_+ + m_-p_-,
\qquad
m_\pm\in\mathbb Z_{\ge 0}.
\]

Faithfulness to the two-center parent requires

\[
m_+\ge 1,\qquad m_-\ge 1.
\]

No extra local ramification multiplicity requires the minimal faithful choice

\[
m_+=m_-=1.
\]

Therefore

\[
R=R_{1,1}=p_+ + p_-,
\]

and

\[
\deg R=2.
\]

This is the exact point where V4 uses more than "there are two marked points":
it uses unit multiplicity at each marked point.  If one center is omitted, the
divisor is not faithful.  If either multiplicity is larger than one, the parent
has acquired extra local ramification data.

## Line-Bundle Lock On \(\mathbb{CP}^1\)

The Picard group of the Riemann sphere is

\[
\mathrm{Pic}(\mathbb{CP}^1)\simeq\mathbb Z,
\]

generated by \(\mathcal O(1)\).  Two divisors on \(\mathbb{CP}^1\) determine
isomorphic line bundles exactly by their degree.  Hence

\[
L_R=\mathcal O(R)
\simeq
\mathcal O(\deg R)
=
\mathcal O(2).
\]

Equivalently,

\[
c_1(L_R)=PD[p_+] + PD[p_-],
\qquad
\int_{\mathbb{CP}^1}c_1(L_R)=2.
\]

Riemann-Roch and Serre duality then give

\[
h^0(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O(2))=3,
\qquad
h^1(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O(2))=0.
\]

## Why Unit Multiplicity Is Necessary

Two-center support alone allows

\[
R_{m_+,m_-}=m_+p_+ + m_-p_-.
\]

The single-center choice \(R=p_+\) has degree one and would give

\[
h^0(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O(1))=2.
\]

A higher-ramification faithful choice such as \(R=2p_+ + p_-\) has degree three
and would give

\[
h^0(\mathbb{CP}^1,\mathcal O(3))=4.
\]

Thus

\[
\mathcal O(2)
\]

is selected neither by the index count alone nor by two marked points alone.
It is selected by the stronger V4 condition:

\[
\boxed{\text{faithful two centers}+\text{unit ramification per center}.}
\]

## V4 Verdict

\[
\boxed{
C=\mathbb{CP}^1,\quad q_+=q_-=1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
